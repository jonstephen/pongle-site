_Version 3, 23 October 2026_

These terms apply to **Public Tables**: places to play that you publish so other signed-in Pongle players can find them. You accept them in the app before you publish a Table, browse Public Tables or add a photo of a Table. If they change, the app asks you again.

## Zero tolerance

No offensive, abusive, hateful or sexual names or notes, and nothing that targets a person. Tables that break this are hidden, and the account that looks after them can be barred from publishing, editing Public Tables or adopting Tables. Names and notes are checked against a word list, and every Report is reviewed **within 24 hours**.

## Publishing is permanent

Once a Table is Public, you can edit it or step down from looking after it, but you can't make it private again or delete it. That way, matches people played there keep their place. If a Table is wrong or gone, report it.

## Homes

Don't publish someone's home without their permission. A Public Table at a private home only ever shows others a rough area and the suburb or town. If you see a home published as something else, report it as "This is a private home".

## Photos

Whoever looks after a Public Table can add one photo of it. The photo must:

- show the table and its surroundings only, with **no people, faces or number plates**;
- contain nothing offensive;
- be one you took, or one you have the right to share.

The moderator approves every photo before anyone else sees it, and can take one down at any time. Photos are never shown for a Table at a private home: making a Table a private home deletes its photo. A photo belongs to the Table, so it stays if you step down or delete your account. To have one taken down, report it as "Problem with the photo" or email us.

## Looking after a Table

Whoever adds a Table looks after it as its **Ambassador**, and is the only one who can edit it or change its photo. If a Public Table has no Ambassador, you can ask to adopt it, and the moderator decides.

## If you delete your account

Tables you published stay listed, with their photos, without any link to you, so other players can still find them.

## Reporting and hiding

On any Public Table you can **Report** it, or just its photo, to the moderator, or **hide all Tables from whoever looks after it** (they aren't told). To report a problem or ask a question, email **[jon.paulsen@gmail.com](mailto:jon.paulsen@gmail.com)**.
