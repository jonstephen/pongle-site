_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Pongle is a scorekeeper for table tennis. This policy explains what Pongle stores, where, and what you can do about it. If you have a question, email **[jon.paulsen@gmail.com](mailto:jon.paulsen@gmail.com)**.

## The short version

- You can use Pongle without an account. Then everything stays on your device.
- If you sign in, your players, matches and Tables are backed up so they sync across your devices.
- Tables you **publish** can be seen by other signed-in people. A Table at a private home only ever shows a rough area to others, never its address.
- A **photo** you add to a Public Table is seen by others only once the moderator approves it, and never shows where it was taken. A photo of a private Table or a home is seen only by you and by whoever accepts a Meetup there.
- If you **accept an Invite** to a player on someone else's list, you both see some of each other's details: they see your name and bat colour, and you see the matches they recorded with that player.
- In a **Two-Phone Match**, the match is held on our servers while it's played, so both phones can show it. Joining one puts each of you on the other's list, as an Invite does.
- A **Meetup** (Play Later) is a Table and a time you plan with someone you know. It's held on our servers until a day after its time. Once they accept, they see exactly where the Table is, even at a home, and each of you is put on the other's list.
- Pongle has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and doesn't sell or share your data.

## Without an account

Your players (names and bat colours), your matches and your settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to Pongle's servers.

## With an account

Signing in is optional and uses your **email address** (we email you a sign-in link). While you're signed in, Pongle stores on its servers:

- **Your players:** their names and bat colours, and which one is you.
- **Your matches:** who played, the points, when they were played, and, if you chose one, the Table they were played at (its name at the time).
- **Your Tables:** the places you play. Each has a name, optional notes, a map pin, the address and suburb or town looked up from that pin, and who owns the physical table (for example "Club or venue" or "Private home"). Nobody else can see your Tables unless you publish them.
- **That you accepted the Table terms**, and when.

## Public Tables

When you **publish** a Table, other signed-in people can see its name, notes, owner kind, map pin and address, plus an anonymous count of matches played there and roughly when it was last played. They can't see who added it or who looks after it.

If the Table is at a **private home**, others only see a rough area (about half a mile) and the suburb or town. They never see its address, its exact pin or its match counts. Publishing a home needs you to confirm it's your home or that you have the homeowner's permission.

Publishing can't be undone. If you delete your account, your published Tables stay listed, without any link to you, so other people's matches there keep their place. Matches recorded at a Public Table add to its anonymous match count.

## Table Photos

You can add **one photo** of a Table you look after, picked from your photo library. Pongle only sees the photo you pick, not the rest of your library.

- **Before it's uploaded**, Pongle makes a new copy of the photo on your device, smaller and without its location, camera details or other hidden information.
- **On a Public Table that isn't at a private home**, the moderator sees it first. Nobody else sees it until the moderator approves it. If it isn't approved, you see why. Once approved, other signed-in people who can see the Table see the photo. They aren't told who added it.
- **On a private Table or a Table at a home**, the moderator doesn't check the photo. Only you see it, and someone who has accepted a Meetup with you there (see Meetups). People browsing a published home never see it. If you publish a private Table, its photo goes to the moderator first.
- **We store** the photo, which Table it's of, which account added it and when, and the moderator's decision.
- **It belongs to the Table.** It stays if you step down from looking after the Table or delete your account, and whoever looks after the Table next can replace or remove it.

**Remove Photo** on your Table takes it down straight away. A photo is also deleted when it's replaced, not approved, taken down by the moderator, or when its Table is deleted. Its file is deleted from our servers within the hour. If a Public Table becomes a private home, its photo stays, but from then on only you and Meetup guests see it. To have any Table's photo taken down, report it as "Problem with the photo" or email us.

## Keeping Public Tables safe

To keep Public Tables free of offensive content:

- Public Table names and notes are checked against a list of offensive words on our server. A Table with a listed word is hidden from others until it's reworded.
- You can **report** a Public Table or its photo, with an optional note. A reported photo is hidden from you straight away. Reports, and requests to look after a Table that has no one, go to the moderator by email.
- You can **hide all Tables from whoever looks after a Table**. They aren't told.

We keep reports, hidden-Table lists, these requests and moderation decisions only as long as we need them to moderate Public Tables.

## Invites and Claims

If you're signed in, you can **invite** the person behind one of your players to claim that player. An Invite is a link, a QR code or a 10-character code. It works once and expires after 7 days, and you can cancel it.

- **Anyone with the Invite**, signed in or not, can see your name, the player's name and how many matches that player has played, until the Invite is used, cancelled or expires.
- **If they accept**, they've claimed that player. You'll then see their own name and bat colour for that player. They'll see the matches you recorded with that player, with your name and the other player's name, and future matches too. They can't change or delete your matches.
- **We store** each Invite (who sent it, for which player, and whether it was accepted, declined or cancelled, and when), each claim (which account claimed which player, and when), and which of your matches you've removed from your history.

If someone claimed one of your players:

- You can't rename that player or change its colour. They choose those.
- You can still delete the player or any match.

If you claimed someone else's player:

- **Remove from my history** hides one of their matches from your history. Their copy is unchanged.
- **Leave** undoes the claim. Their matches with that player leave your history, and they see that player as they had it before.

## Two-Phone Matches

If you're signed in, you and another signed-in person can score one match on two phones, each scoring your own points. One of you starts it and shows a QR code (a **Join code**); the other scans it to **join**.

- **While it's played**, the match is held on our servers: the points, who serves first, the scoring rules, the Table if one was chosen, and when each phone was last seen. Only the two of you can see it. When it ends, it's kept like any other match, in the history of whoever started it.
- **Anyone who scans the Join code**, signed in or not, can see the name of whoever started the match, until a phone joins.
- **Joining** links each of you to a player on the other's list, as accepting an Invite does (see above). Each phone asks which of its players, if any, is the other person. If the chosen player already has matches, the other person confirms they were them before those matches count for them. Otherwise they get a new player. You can **Leave** these later, as with any claim.
- **We store** each phone's answers while you join, and remove the live match a day after it ends.

## Meetups

If you're signed in, **Play Later** plans a Meetup: a Table, a time and one person from your players, or someone new by name. Pongle doesn't look anyone up. You send them a link, and a calendar file, yourself, for example in Messages.

- **Anyone with the link**, signed in or not, can see your name, the name you gave them, the time, and the Table's name and town or suburb. Not its address.
- **If they accept**, they see the Table's exact location and address, and its photo if it has one, even if it's your home or a private Table, and only through that Meetup. Each of you is linked to a player on the other's list, as accepting an Invite does (see above).
- **If they decline**, or say later they can't make it, you see that.
- **We store** each Meetup (who planned it, for which player, the Table, the time, the answer and when it changed) until a day after its time, then delete it.
- **Calendar:** Add to Calendar opens your phone's own event editor, filled in. Pongle doesn't read your calendar or ask for access to it.

## Camera

Pongle uses the camera only when you tap **Scan a Join Code**, to read the QR code. Nothing the camera sees is stored or sent anywhere.

## Location

Pongle asks for your location only when you tap **Use my location** or the location button on the Tables map. It uses it to place a Table's pin or to show Tables near you. Your location isn't stored or sent anywhere, except as the pin of a Table you choose to save.

## Services Pongle uses

- **Supabase** hosts Pongle's database and Table photos, and handles sign-in.
- **Resend** delivers the moderator's emails. These contain report details and Table names, not your email address.
- **Apple Maps** looks up addresses and searches for places when you add a Table.
- **Apple Health** receives workouts you choose to record on Apple Watch. They stay in your Health data and aren't sent to Pongle's servers.

## Deleting your data

- **Sign Out** (in Account) clears your account's data from that device, once it's backed up.
- **Delete Account** (in Account) deletes your account and, from our servers, your players, matches, Two-Phone Matches you started, private Tables and their photos, Invites, claims, reports, hidden-Table lists, requests and terms acceptance. Your Meetups are cancelled, so the other person sees that they're off, and deleted a day after their time. Your published Tables stay listed, with their photos, without any link to you. To take a photo down first, use Remove Photo.
  - Matches you recorded also leave the history of anyone who claimed one of your players.
  - Any player you claimed goes back to how its owner had it.
- Without an account, deleting the app deletes everything.

You can also email **[jon.paulsen@gmail.com](mailto:jon.paulsen@gmail.com)** to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

## Children

Accounts and Public Tables aren't meant for children under 13. Children can use Pongle without signing in.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If the change matters, you'll be asked to accept the Table terms again in the app.
