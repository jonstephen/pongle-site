Questions, problems or feedback: email **[jon.paulsen@gmail.com](mailto:jon.paulsen@gmail.com)**. We reply as soon as we can, and act on reports about Public Tables within 24 hours.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Pongle keeps score without one, and everything stays on your device. Sign in (Account, in the settings dock) to back up your players, matches and Tables, and to use Public Tables.

**How do I report a Public Table?**
Open the Table and tap **Report…**. You can also hide all Tables from whoever looks after it.

**My Table's name was flagged, but it's a real place.**
Word filters sometimes catch real place names. Email us the Table's name and where it is, and we'll allow it.

**Can I unpublish a Table?**
No, publishing is permanent, so the matches played there keep their place. You can edit it, or step down from looking after it. If it's wrong or gone, report it.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the settings dock, open **Account** and tap **Delete Account**. This deletes your players, matches, private Tables and other account data from our servers. Tables you published stay listed without any link to you. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ "/privacy/" | relative_url }}).

**My Flic button or Apple Watch isn't scoring.**
Check Input Device Options in the settings dock. Flic buttons need Bluetooth on, and the Watch needs the Pongle Watch app installed.
